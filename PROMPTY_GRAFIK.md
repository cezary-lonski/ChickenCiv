# Prompty do generowania grafik (Chicken Civ)

Styl wspólny dla wszystkich grafik (dopisz na końcu każdego promptu):
`stylized low-poly game art, bright saturated colors, soft cartoon lighting, clean outlines, cute farm-fantasy "chicken civilization" theme, consistent with a cozy 3D strategy game, no text, no watermark`

Format: ikony zasobów 512x512 na przezroczystym tle (PNG); tła bitwy 1920x1080 (16:9).

## Ikony surowców (po jednej grafice na surowiec)

1. **Pszenica (Wheat)** – `a bundle of golden wheat ears tied with a red ribbon, isometric game resource icon, centered, transparent background`
2. **Bydło (Cattle)** – `a cute brown and white cow standing on a small grass tile, isometric game resource icon, centered, transparent background`
3. **Ryby (Fish)** – `two shiny blue fish jumping over small waves, isometric game resource icon, centered, transparent background`
4. **Konie (Horses)** – `a chestnut horse with a flowing mane standing on a small grass tile, isometric game resource icon, centered, transparent background`
5. **Żelazo (Iron)** – `a pile of dark grey iron ore chunks with metallic highlights and a pickaxe leaning on it, isometric game resource icon, centered, transparent background`
6. **Kamień (Stone)** – `a stack of light grey cut stone blocks and a few rough boulders, isometric game resource icon, centered, transparent background`
7. **Złoto (Gold)** – `a small heap of shiny gold nuggets and coins with sparkles, isometric game resource icon, centered, transparent background`
8. **Nuggetsy (waluta bitewna)** – `a golden chicken nugget coin with a tiny crown embossed on it, game currency icon, centered, transparent background`

## Tła bitew (plansza walki, widok z góry pod kątem, miejsce na heksową siatkę)

Do każdego dodaj: `wide landscape, empty open ground in the center for a tactical hex grid, nothing important in the middle, battlefield backdrop, 1920x1080`

- **Łąka** – `green meadow with scattered flowers, a wooden fence and a distant red barn, sunny sky with fluffy clouds`
- **Las** – `dense stylized pine forest clearing, tall trees framing the edges, soft sunlight rays, mossy ground`
- **Góry** – `rocky mountain pass with grey cliffs and snowy peaks in the distance, gravel ground, scattered boulders`
- **Wzgórza** – `rolling green hills with rocks and tufts of grass, a winding dirt path, blue sky`
- **Pustynia** – `sandy desert with dunes, cracked dry ground, a few cacti and rocks, hot sun and orange sky`
- **Wybrzeże** – `sandy beach battlefield with shallow turquoise water on one side, palm trees, small waves`
- **Pole farmy** – `harvested farm field with hay bales, a scarecrow and a wooden windmill far away, autumn light`
- **Miasto (oblężenie)** – `stone city walls and wooden gates with banners at the top edge, cobblestone ground, torches`
- **Kosmos / stacja** – `sci-fi space station hangar floor with glowing panels and stars visible through large windows`
- **Gwiazda Śmierci** – `huge grey spherical battle station looming in a starfield, metallic floor with trench lines in the foreground`

## Teren na mapie (alternatywa dla modeli 3D, tekstury kafli 1024x1024, bezszwowe)
